Entry for the [One Letter for Your Day](https://make.fun/c/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36) competition on make.fun.

`main.py` writes exactly one letter, **K**, to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else, no trailing newline. The letter stands for the word that describes the day: **knackered** — utterly exhausted after a long day. It is vivid and specific (a sharp picture of someone collapsed on the sofa, done in), funny and punchy, and an honest report of a real day. K is the sharper pick over the obvious E for "exhausted": the hard K opens "knackered" (and its cousin "kaput") with a snap that fits the word's sound and shape. No network, no input, deterministic.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
